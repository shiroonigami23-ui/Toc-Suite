Privacy policy

Toc-Suite is a study tool. It has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies. Almost everything you do stays in your own browser.

What stays on your device

DataWhereWhy
Display name, theme, text size, motion and animation preferencesBrowser localStorage (keys starting with toc)To remember your settings
The machine on each studio's canvasBrowser localStorageSo a refresh does not lose your work
Files you save or export (JSON, PNG, CSV)Your downloads folderThey are created in the browser and never uploaded
The English → DFA model, practice banks and librariesDownloaded with the appThey run entirely in your browser

What is sent to a server, and when

  • Assignment and quiz submissions. When you choose to submit a save to an open assignment, the app sends your name, your college email and roll number, the machine, the assignment id and a coarse device signature (browser, language, time zone, screen size) to the course server (Netlify Functions with a Postgres database) so the instructor can review it and detect duplicate submissions.
  • Sharing ordinary saves (optional). By default, a normal save is also copied to the class review queue with your name (and roll number if you logged in). You can turn this off in Settings → Data and Privacy.

Retention and your choices

  • Local data stays until you erase it: Settings → Data and Privacy → Erase All Local Data, or clear site data in your browser.
  • Submitted work is kept for the duration of the course. To have a submission removed, contact the maintainer (below).
  • The app does not sell, rent or share data with anyone other than the services named above.

Terms of use

  • Toc-Suite is open-source software under the MIT License. It is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
  • It is a learning aid. Solutions, hints and automatically generated machines are checked carefully but can still be wrong; if your instructor's answer differs, ask them, and please report the problem.
  • For quizzes and graded assignments, follow your instructor's academic-integrity rules. Using the Automata Assistant or "Show Solution" during a graded task may not be allowed.
  • Do not use the submission system to send content that is not your own work, or anything offensive or unrelated to the course.
  • Do not try to overload or break the course server (for example by scripting thousands of submissions).

Accessibility

Toc-Suite aims to be usable on phones, tablets and desktops, with a keyboard, a mouse or touch.

  • Keyboard: press ? in any studio for the full list of shortcuts (tools, undo/redo, save, open, export, zoom, run).
  • Motion: choose Reduced Motion or an animation speed in Settings; the app also follows your operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
  • Text size: Normal, Large and Extra Large in Settings.
  • Small screens: the side panel becomes a drawer and the canvas scrolls instead of shrinking.

Known limitation: drawing on the canvas needs a pointer (mouse, pen or touch). The English → DFA box, the examples learner and the practice checker work fully from the keyboard and produce the machine for you.
